02 · Consulting

Streams that drain, jobs that finish.

Data platforms slow down in predictable places: partitions chosen years ago, skewed joins, small files, lag nobody watches. We find the bottleneck with your metrics, fix it with your team and leave the monitoring that shows it stays fixed.

01

Streaming architecture

Topic and partition design, keys and ordering, retention and compaction, schema registry, exactly-once.

You getA reviewed event model and a capacity plan.

02

Kafka operations

KRaft migration, broker sizing, rack awareness, replication and min ISR, rebalancing storms, MirrorMaker.

You getTuned clusters and lag alerts that mean something.

03

Spark performance

Skew, shuffles, broadcast joins and salting, partitioning, AQE, executor memory, small files.

You getFaster, cheaper jobs with before/after run times.

04

Lakehouse & warehouse

Parquet, Iceberg and Delta tables, partitioning, the Hive metastore and Trino for interactive SQL.

You getA table layout that queries fast and costs less.

05

Integration & CDC

Kafka Connect, Debezium change data capture, sinks into search, vector and warehouse stores; RabbitMQ bridges.

You getPipelines with replay, dead-letter queues and monitoring.

06

Platform on Kubernetes

Strimzi for Kafka, the Spark operator and Trino on Kubernetes; autoscaling and cost control.

You getA reproducible platform defined as code.

Signals it is time to call

  • Consumer lag grows every evening and drains by morning
  • One Spark task runs for an hour while the rest take minutes
  • The nightly batch no longer finishes before the workday
  • Partition counts were picked years ago and never revisited
  • Millions of small files slow every query
  • ZooKeeper to KRaft is on the roadmap but nobody owns it

How an engagement runs

  1. Scoping call 45 min · free

    We hear the problem, agree goals and the numbers success is judged by.

  2. Assessment 1–2 weeks

    Read-only access, diagnostics, logs and short interviews. Findings in writing.

  3. Fix & tune 2–6 weeks

    Changes made with your team in small steps, each measured before and after.

  4. Hand-over ongoing

    Runbooks, a focused workshop for your engineers and optional retained support.
