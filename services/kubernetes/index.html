02 · Consulting

Clusters that are secure, observable and boring.

Most Kubernetes trouble is not Kubernetes: it is unclear ownership, hand-made changes and missing limits. We bring structure — code, policies, dashboards — and leave a team that understands why each piece is there.

01

Cluster design & setup

kubeadm, RKE2, K3s or managed; CNI networking, storage classes, ingress and multi-cluster layouts.

You getA reproducible cluster built from code.

02

Security hardening

RBAC, Pod Security Standards, NetworkPolicies, secrets, image scanning and admission policies.

You getFindings mapped to CIS benchmarks, and the fixes applied.

03

Delivery & GitOps

Helm and Kustomize, Argo CD or Flux, CI/CD pipelines, progressive rollouts and rollback.

You getDeployments that are reviewed, repeatable and reversible.

04

Observability

Prometheus, Grafana and Alertmanager; logs with ELK, OpenSearch or Loki; SLOs.

You getDashboards and alerts that point to causes.

05

Stateful workloads

Operators for Elasticsearch, Kafka, Spark, Trino, Redis and Qdrant; storage, backups and upgrades.

You getData services that survive the loss of a node.

06

Cost & capacity

Requests and limits, HPA and VPA, cluster autoscaling, node pools and spot capacity.

You getA smaller bill without OOM kills.

Signals it is time to call

  • Pods get OOMKilled or sit in Pending
  • Upgrades are postponed because nobody knows what breaks
  • Everyone is cluster-admin
  • Deploys are kubectl apply from someone’s laptop
  • Alerts fire constantly and nobody acts on them
  • Databases on Kubernetes have never had a restore test

How an engagement runs

  1. Scoping call 45 min · free

    We hear the problem, agree goals and the numbers success is judged by.

  2. Assessment 1–2 weeks

    Read-only access, diagnostics, logs and short interviews. Findings in writing.

  3. Fix & tune 2–6 weeks

    Changes made with your team in small steps, each measured before and after.

  4. Hand-over ongoing

    Runbooks, a focused workshop for your engineers and optional retained support.

03 · Training

From the first pod to production practice.

Four days for teams new to Kubernetes, and a five-day follow-on for running data platforms on it. Every topic is practised on a cluster per participant.

Programme 01 · Core

Kubernetes

4 days · 24 hours
Level
Beginner – intermediate
Prereq.
Basic Linux and containers
  • Day 1 · Foundations. Docker and containers, microservices, Kubernetes architecture (control plane, workers, pods, ReplicaSets, Services); installing with Minikube, kubeadm, K3s, Kubespray, RKE2 or Rancher; the Dashboard
  • Day 2 · Workloads. Pods and inter-container communication, stateful and stateless apps, Deployments, Services, ConfigMaps and Secrets, updates and rollback
  • Day 3 · Scale & security. Logging and debugging, scaling and load balancing, RBAC, pod security, NetworkPolicies, monitoring
  • Day 4 · Operations. Prometheus and Grafana, ELK logging, alerting, CI/CD integration, Helm packages and deployment automation
